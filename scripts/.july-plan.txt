READ THIS FIRST
The outcome: a production document, not a
list of ideas
Each of the 120 briefs contains five silent on-screen copy beats, five timed visual beats, a unique demonstration
system, Remotion implementation cues, low instrumental music and sound direction, a fact guard, and exactly five
hashtags. Every reel is explicitly voice-free and header-free. The plan is engineered for retention and discussion. No
document can guarantee virality; execution, distribution, account history and timing still affect reach.
One non-negotiable
The visual must explain the claim even with audio muted. Text labels identify moving evidence; they do not replace it. If a scene can be
produced as only a title on a background, the scene has failed this brief. Keep the top 180 px empty: no ID, category, logo, timer,
progress bar or any other fixed header element.
Retention architecture
+ 0-3 seconds - Promise: show the contradiction or finished consequence immediately.
+ 3-8 seconds - Curiosity: introduce the mechanism, conflict or missing explanation.
+ 8-15 seconds - Progress: demonstrate the system changing with objects, data or UI.
+ 15-24 seconds - Payoff: reveal the counterpoint or the cost hidden by the hook.
+ 24-30 seconds - New curiosity: hold the verdict, then ask one polarising but answerable question.
Viral quality filter
+ The hook must be understandable from frame zero without sound.
+ Every on-screen copy beat must change the viewer's knowledge or expectation.
+ At least one visible state change must occur before second 5.
+ Pattern interrupts are motivated by a reveal, not dropped in on a timer.
+ The final frame must be a designed composition, not a fading caption.
+ Debate prompts ask viewers to choose or explain; they never harass, defame or fabricate.

REMOTION PRODUCTION CONTRACT
Technical specification for every reel
Use one typed, parameterised composition with data-driven scenes, but give each brief a distinct layout, palette,
camera grammar, transition language and sound motif. Shared code is encouraged; repeated creative templates are
not.
PROPERTY LOCKED SPECIFICATION
Canvas 1080 x 1920 portrait, square pixels, 30 fps, exactly 900 frames (30.0 seconds).
Delivery H.264 MP4, High profile, yuv420p, CRF 16-18; AAC 48 kHz. Verify an audio stream exists.
Safe area Keep key content 90 px from left, 170 px from right and 320 px from bottom. Reserve the top 180 px as a
completely empty overlay zone.
Speech None. Do not generate, record or import voice, voiceover, narration, dialogue, presenter audio, TTS, chanting,
spoken samples or vocals.
On-screen copy Use the five written beats as silent kinetic text. Break them into 2-6 word units, highlight one term, keep high
contrast and never cover the demonstration.
Music Every reel has a licensed/original instrumental-only bed at 0.06-0.12 volume. Keep it low, supportive and free of
vocals.
Effects Every reel has restrained UI, impact, travel or mechanism cues; no clipping and no abrupt tail.
Header overlay Forbidden. Never render D01A or any brief ID, category, series label, logo, timer, countdown, clock, progress bar
or fixed text at the top. The document ID is organisational only.
Asset rule Local SVG, Lottie, generated textures, maps and licensed audio through staticFile(). No expiring URLs.
Exclusions No people, human silhouettes, hands, heads, chalk, chalkboards, grid backgrounds or text-only scenes.
Canonical data model
type ReelSpec = { id; title; durationInFrames: 900; scenes: SceneSpec[]; copy: CopyCue[]; instrumentalMusic; sfx; evidence;
theme; seed; showHeader: false; speechTrack: null; };
+ Drive all motion from useCurrentFrame() and useVideoConfig().
+ Use Series or TransitionSeries for scene timing; lock copy reveals to the five specified time ranges.
+ Use clamped interpolate(), settled spring() and seeded random() only.
+ Use local SVG, @remotion/shapes, Lottie, ThreeCanvas, SkiaCanvas and MapLibre/Turf when the brief calls for them.
+ Use @remotion/media Audio only for instrumental music and SFX, with frame-based volume curves. Never add a speech, TTS, dialogue or
vocal track.
+ Render the supplied copy as designed kinetic text, not as a transcript. Keep every text element below the empty top zone.
+ Never use CSS transitions, CSS keyframes, wall-clock timing, Math.random(), missing remote assets or render-time network
dependencies.

CREATIVE SYSTEM
Visual, motion, type and audio grammar
These rules translate the reference images into a repeatable production standard while preserving the required
uniqueness of all 120 reels.
Object-first composition
Every scene contains a meaningful object: chip, server, switch, gauge, orbit, map, contract, meter, file, graph, device, planet, lock,
pipeline or UI state. A caption cannot be the hero.
One visual job per shot
Use claim to evidence, problem to mechanism, comparison to verdict. Do not
advanced.
Typography
Use a high-impact sans for hooks, a clear sans for labels, a mono for code and telemetry, and a serif only for deliberate editorial
contrast. Avoid more than three families per reel.
Camera
Use purposeful pushes, pans, parallax and orbital moves. Settle the camera du
handheld motion or motion that competes with reading.
Music
Choose an instrumental bed matched to the claim: restrained pulse, modular synth, minimal piano, percussive click-bed, ambient
tension or cinematic sub. Keep it low, instrumental and free of vocals.
Sound design
Tie effects to causes: packet ping, latch, relay, meter tick, soft alarm, orbit pas
spam and excessive whooshes.
Mix target: no speech or vocal track; overall programme around -14 LUFS integrated as a practical social target; true
peak below -1 dBTP; instrumental music low and supportive; effects short, clear and restrained. Check on headphones
and a phone speaker.

A reel is not complete until it passes these
checks
Render representative frames before the final export. Review the opening, every transition, the densest caption, the
central demonstration, the verdict and the final frame.
GATE PASS CONDITION
Narrative Frame zero communicates tension; every scene advances; payoff is visible long enough; CTA is one answerable
question.
Visual No blank frames, clipping, unreadable type, fake data, low-resolution assets, social UI overlap or prohibited
imagery.
Motion All motion is deterministic; interpolation clamps; springs settle; scene boundaries do not flash or duplicate.
Audio There is no voice, voiceover, narration, dialogue, TTS or vocal music; instrumental music is low; SFX align with
actions; no clipping or abrupt endings.
On-screen copy Text is accurate, timed, readable and inside the safe zone; one highlight is enough; it is never treated as a
narration transcript.
Header The top 180 px contains no fixed text or UI: no document ID, category, series label, logo, timer, countdown, clock
or progress bar.
Technical Exactly 30.0 seconds, 1080 x 1920, 30 fps, H.264 video, AAC audio; file plays from first to last frame.
Batch Input row validates; filename is deterministic; failed renders do not delete completed outputs; manifest records
status.
Editorial Time-sensitive facts are rechecked; allegations are attributed; the thumbnail and title do not promise a different
video.
Required still checks: frames 0, 45, 90, 240, 450, 720 and 899. Also render a low-resolution 0-8 second draft for hook
timing and a 14-25 second draft for the evidence-to-verdict transition.
